# How to Reframe Customer Agent → CRM / VIP (Without Lying)

Recruiters for VIP Account Manager and CRM roles care about **relationship ownership**, **data in CRM**, **retention**, and **revenue protection** — not whether your badge said "Customer Agent."

## What you likely already did (use these words)

| You probably did this… | Say it like this on CV/LinkedIn |
|------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Helped regular big depositors | Managed high-value / VIP player relationships |
| Wrote notes after every chat | Maintained accurate CRM documentation & case history |
| Called players who hadn't played | Proactive reactivation & retention outreach |
| Fixed bonus / payment issues | End-to-end case ownership & escalation |
| Checked IDs and documents | Supported KYC/verification workflows |
| Applied deposit limits / SE | Responsible gaming compliance & player safeguarding |
| Passed chats to fraud/payments | Cross-functional escalation with full player context |
| Hit QA scores | Quality & SLA performance in regulated environment |
| Used tags / segments in system | Player segmentation & lifecycle tracking in CRM |

## Game Presenter — don't undersell it

Presenting is **live account-facing work**:
- Real-time rapport with players (many VIPs play live tables)
- Brand representation under pressure
- Spotting dissatisfaction before it becomes a complaint
- Working with ops/support without breaking the experience

Frame it as **Player Engagement & Live Relationship Management**, not entertainment only.

## Title strategy

- **CV header / LinkedIn headline:** Lead with target role (VIP / CRM / Retention)
- **Job titles in experience:** Keep official title + subtitle in parentheses if needed  
  e.g. `Customer Agent (VIP & CRM Operations)`
- **Never invent** a job title your employer didn't give you on formal references — subtitles and descriptions are enough

## Remote & Thailand angle

Many iGaming CRM/VIP roles are **remote within EU/time zones** or based in **Malta, Cyprus, Gibraltar, Isle of Man, Curacao-licensed operators**.

Thailand itself has fewer regulated iGaming HQs, but **remote roles** from EU operators are realistic if you have:
- Stable internet
- Overlap with EU business hours (partial overlap is often OK)
- Right to work / tax clarity (research Thai visa options: DTV, employer-sponsored, etc.)

Search terms:
- "VIP Account Manager remote iGaming"
- "Player Retention Specialist remote"
- "CRM iGaming Malta remote"
- "Customer Success gaming fintech"
