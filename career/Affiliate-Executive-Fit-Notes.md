## Metrics to learn before interview (15 min Google)

- **FTD** — First Time Deposit  
- **CPA** — Cost Per Acquisition  
- **Rev Share** — % of NGR paid to affiliate  
- **Hybrid** — CPA + Rev Share mix  
- **NGR** — Net Gaming Revenue  
- **Postback / S2S** — conversion tracking signal to affiliate platform  
- **Traffic quality** — players who convert and stay, not just clicks
