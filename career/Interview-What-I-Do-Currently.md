# Interview Answer — "What do you do currently?"

Use this script. Practice it until it feels natural (60–90 seconds).

---

## The short version (if they want a quick answer)

> "I work in casino operations and product support at Glitnor. My official title is Senior Customer Agent, but day to day I'm not doing basic frontline support — I configure promotions, manage product issues in Jira, support player journeys across multiple regulated markets including Sweden, Ontario, and the Netherlands, and coordinate with CRM, VIP, payments, and compliance. It's much closer to an operations and product support role than a traditional CS agent role."

---

## The full version (recommended)

> "I'm currently at Glitnor in a Senior Customer Agent role, but my day-to-day work sits between **casino operations, product support, and cross-team coordination** — which is actually why this position appealed to me.
>
> On a typical day, I'm not just answering player emails. I **review, set up, and configure promotions and bonus campaigns**, making sure configurations are correct before and after go-live. I use **Jira daily** to raise and track product bugs, improvement requests, and operational issues, and I document processes in **Confluence**. I also work in **Intercom daily** for player-facing operational queries.
>
> A big part of my role is understanding the **full player journey** — registration, deposits, bonuses, gameplay, withdrawals — and I do this across **multiple regulated markets**: Malta, **Swedish SGA**, **Ontario**, and the **Dutch KSA** market, not just standard MGA. Each market has different bonus rules, RG requirements, and compliance processes, so I need to know how the platform behaves differently in each.
>
> I work closely with **CRM, VIP, Customer Support, payments, compliance, and product** teams. When something breaks — a promo misconfiguration, a payment issue, a product bug — I'm often the person who spots it first, documents it, escalates it, and follows it through.
>
> So while my title says Customer Agent, the work is really **casino operations and product support** — which is exactly the direction I want to move in."

---

## If they push: "So why isn't your title higher?"

> "That's a fair question. In iGaming, especially in Malta, companies often keep broader operational roles under Customer Support job titles even when the work goes well beyond it. My responsibilities grew into promotion configuration, Jira product escalation, and multi-market operations, but the formal title didn't change. I'm now actively looking to move into a role that reflects the work I'm already doing — which is why this Casino Operations & Product Specialist position is a great fit."

**Stay calm and factual. Don't sound bitter about the title.**

---

## If they ask for a specific example (prepare one of each)

### Promo configuration
> "Recently I [set up / reviewed] a [bonus type] campaign for [market — e.g. Ontario/SGA]. I checked the wagering, eligibility, and player-facing flow before go-live, spotted [issue], flagged it to [team], and we fixed it before it affected players."

### Jira / product bug
> "A player reported [issue] affecting [deposits/bonuses/gameplay]. I reproduced it, raised a Jira ticket with full steps, tagged product/payments, tracked it daily, and followed up until resolved. I also updated Confluence so the team had the fix documented."

### Multi-market regulatory
> "Working across SGA and Dutch markets, bonus rules and RG limits work very differently from MGA. For example, [specific difference you know — e.g. SGA deposit limits, Dutch CRUKS, Ontario fit-to-play]. I apply that knowledge daily when handling promos and player queries."

## Words to USE vs AVOID

| Say this | Avoid this |
|----------|------------|
| Casino operations | "I'm just customer support" |
| Product support | "I only answer chats" |
| Promotion configuration | "I don't really do product" |
| Multi-market regulatory (SGA, Ontario, KSA) | "I'm only Malta" |
| Jira / operational tooling | Downplaying your tools |
| Cross-team coordination | Making it sound entry-level |

---

## On your CV vs official title

- **CV title:** Casino Operations & Product Support *(describes the work)*
- **Subtitle:** Senior Customer Agent *(honest for reference checks)*
- **In interview:** Lead with what you **do**, then mention official title if asked

This is standard in iGaming. Recruiters understand titles lag behind responsibilities.
