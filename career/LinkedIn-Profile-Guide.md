# LinkedIn Profile — CRM / VIP Positioning Guide

Use this alongside your CV. LinkedIn should tell the same story but in a more conversational, searchable way.

---

## 1. Headline (220 characters max)

**Do not use:** "Customer Service Agent" or "Game Presenter" alone.

**Use one of these patterns:**

```
VIP-Focused Player Relations | CRM & Retention | iGaming | Open to Remote
```

```
iGaming CRM & VIP Support Specialist | 4+ Years Malta | Remote / Relocation
```

```
Player Retention & Account Management | Live Casino & CS | CRM-Driven iGaming
```

---

## 2. About / Summary (first 3 lines matter most)

Template:

> I help iGaming operators protect revenue and player trust by managing high-value relationships, CRM-driven follow-ups, and complex case resolution across payments, compliance, and retention.
>
> Over 4 years in Malta's regulated iGaming sector, I've moved from high-volume customer operations into live game presenting — building real-time rapport with international players while staying sharp on bonuses, KYC, responsible gaming, and escalation workflows.
>
> **What I bring:** VIP-style account handling, accurate CRM documentation, proactive reactivation outreach, and calm de-escalation under pressure.
>
> **Looking for:** Remote or international roles in VIP Account Management, CRM, Player Retention, or Customer Success within iGaming or adjacent fintech.
>
> **Open to:** Thailand, EU remote, MENA hubs, fully remote teams.
>
> 📩 Open to messages from recruiters and hiring managers.

---

## 3. Experience — rewrite job titles carefully

LinkedIn titles are searchable. You can stay honest while emphasizing CRM:

| Actual title | LinkedIn title option (if accurate) |
|--------------|-------------------------------------|
| Customer Agent | **Customer Agent — VIP & CRM Operations** |
| Customer Support | **Player Support Specialist (VIP / Retention)** |
| Game Presenter | **Live Game Presenter — Player Engagement** |

In each role description, use the **same bullet structure as your CV** (CRM, VIP, retention, KYC, escalation).

---

## 4. Skills (pin top 3)

1. Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
2. Account Management
3. iGaming / Online Gaming

Add 10–15 more: Player Retention, VIP Management, KYC, Responsible Gaming, Conflict Resolution, Zendesk [your tool], Live Casino, Cross-functional Collaboration, etc.

Ask colleagues for endorsements on **CRM** and **Account Management**, not only "Customer Service."

---

## 5. Featured section

Add if possible:
- A professional photo (neutral background, friendly, business-casual)
- PDF of your CV (once finalized)
- Any certificates (RG, AML internal training, CRM tool)

---

## 6. Open to Work

Turn on **Open to Work** (visible to recruiters only if you prefer):
- **Titles:** VIP Account Manager, CRM Specialist, Player Retention Manager, Customer Success Manager, Account Manager
- **Locations:** Remote, Thailand, Malta, UAE, Cyprus, Gibraltar, etc.
- **Job types:** Full-time, Contract

---

## 7. Keywords recruiters search

Sprinkle naturally in headline, about, and experience:

`VIP` `account manager` `CRM` `retention` `iGaming` `player lifecycle` `KYC` `high value` `remote` `Malta` `casino` `sportsbook`

## 8. Weekly habits (15 min)

- Connect with iGaming recruiters (Malta, Cyprus, Gibraltar, remote-first operators)
- Comment thoughtfully on posts from operators you want to work for
- Apply with CV + short note referencing VIP/CRM experience explicitly
